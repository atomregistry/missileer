Episode Fate Flow

  1. Episode is filmed and locked.
  2. Casualty manifest hash is committed on-chain.
  3. Episode airs and role deaths are revealed.
  4. Matching escrowed pUSDC role-claims are selected.
  5. The pUSDC value is redeemed; the role becomes Fallen.
  6. A non-redeemable Legacy NFT of the same role is issued.
  7. The Legacy NFT becomes eligible for future game utility.

The Game

The claim dies. The legacy survives.

Legacy NFTs are not pUSDC claims. They are MISSILEER universe assets designed for the survival/base-building game: role class, origin episode, fate, traits, faction, and mission eligibility.

Playable Survivors

Legacy NFTs can become playable archetypes: scouts, engineers, defenders, commanders, and legendary operators.

Episode Missions

Episode-origin Legacy NFTs can unlock special missions tied to the event that created them.

Base Building

Roles affect survival loops: supplies, defense, power, reconnaissance, repairs, and surface recovery.
